# Code of conduct

We want a useful, respectful project where people can contribute regardless of background, experience, identity or disability.

Be considerate and specific. Critique code and ideas without attacking people. Welcome questions, acknowledge uncertainty, and keep disagreement focused on evidence. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, sexualize discussions, publish private information, or continue unwanted contact.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, restrict participation, or temporarily or permanently ban participants. Enforcement should be proportional to the behavior and protect the affected person's privacy.

Report concerns privately through GitHub's reporting tools or ask a maintainer for a private channel without posting sensitive details. A maintainer who is involved in the concern should recuse themselves when another maintainer is available. Reports and appeals will be considered in good faith; the project does not promise a fixed response time.
